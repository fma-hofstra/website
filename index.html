About Us

Where Ambition Meets Opportunity

The Financial Management Association at Hofstra University is the premier student-run organization dedicated to bridging the gap between classroom learning and the world of finance. As a nationally recognized chapter of FMA International, we connect students with industry professionals, provide hands-on experience, and prepare members for successful careers on Wall Street and beyond.

Whether you're interested in investment banking, asset management, corporate finance, or fintech, FMA Hofstra gives you the tools, network, and confidence to stand out.
